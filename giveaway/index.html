How to enter · In person only

Four simple steps.

  1. Come see us in person

    All entries are made in person at Tabbris — 1300 South Boulevard, South End (Charlotte) — during Friends & Foils trade nights. No online or mail-in entry.

  2. Grab your free entry

    Fill out a free entry slip in person — no purchase necessary. One free entry per person. This free entry has the same odds as any paid one.

  3. Want more chances? (optional)

    You can buy additional entries at $1 each, up to $10 in paid entries per person (10 max). Totally optional — buying entries never improves the odds of a single entry.

  4. We draw the winner

    One winner is drawn at random on Thursday, September 17, 2026 — the trade night right after the 30th Anniversary set drops on the 16th. We’ll notify the winner by email or in person. You must be present to enter, but you do not need to be present to win.

No purchase necessary
