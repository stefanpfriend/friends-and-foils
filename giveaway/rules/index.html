Official Rules

30th Anniversary Pokémon Elite Trainer Box Giveaway · Last updated August 10, 2026

1. No purchase necessary

NO PURCHASE OR PAYMENT OF ANY KIND IS NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A purchase or payment will not improve your chances of winning. Free entries and paid entries are pooled together and have equal odds of being drawn.

2. Sponsor

This giveaway (the “Giveaway”) is sponsored by Friend Tech Ventures (“Sponsor”), doing business as Friends & Foils, 1300 South Boulevard, Suite B28, Charlotte, NC 28203. The Giveaway is administered by the Sponsor.

3. Eligibility

Open to individuals who are at least 13 years of age at the time of entry and who enter in person as described below. There is no residency restriction. Entrants under 18 must have permission from a parent or legal guardian to enter; if a winner is under 18, the prize will be awarded to the winner’s parent or legal guardian on the minor’s behalf. Employees of the Sponsor and their immediate family and household members are not eligible. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

4. Entry period

The Giveaway begins at the first Friends & Foils trade night on August 19, 2026 and ends at the close of the Friends & Foils trade night on Thursday, September 17, 2026 — the day after the Pokémon 30th Anniversary set launches on September 16, 2026 (the “Entry Period”), local time. Entries submitted before or after the Entry Period will not be eligible.

5. How to enter

All entries must be made in person at Tabbris, 1300 South Boulevard, South End, Charlotte, NC, during Friends & Foils trade nights within the Entry Period. There is no online, mail-in, or phone entry.

  • Free entry (no purchase necessary): Complete a free entry slip in person with the required information. One (1) free entry per person.
  • Paid additional entries (optional): You may obtain additional entries for $1.00 each, up to a maximum of $10.00 in paid entries per person for the entire Giveaway. Purchasing entries is entirely optional and does not increase the odds of any single entry being selected.

You must be present in person to enter. You do not need to be present at the drawing to win. All entries become the property of the Sponsor. Entries that are incomplete, illegible, or that do not comply with these rules are void.

6. Odds of winning

Each entry — whether free or paid — has an equal chance of being selected. The odds of winning depend on the total number of eligible entries received during the Entry Period.

7. Winner selection & drawing

One (1) winner will be selected in a random drawing from all eligible entries on or about Thursday, September 17, 2026, at the Friends & Foils trade night. The drawing will be conducted by the Sponsor as a random drawing of eligible entry slips at the shop. The decisions of the Sponsor are final and binding.

8. Winner notification & claim

The winner will be notified by email and/or in person using the information provided at entry. The winner must claim the prize in person at Tabbris, 1300 South Boulevard, South End, Charlotte, NC within 30 days of notification and may be required to show valid photo ID (or, for a minor, a parent/legal guardian must claim on their behalf). If the winner cannot be contacted, does not respond, or does not claim the prize within that period, the prize may be forfeited and an alternate winner drawn.

9. Prize

One (1) prize: a sealed 30th Anniversary Pokémon Elite Trainer Box. Approximate retail value (“ARV”): $59.99. Only one (1) prize will be awarded. The prize is non-transferable and no cash or other substitution is offered, except that the Sponsor reserves the right to substitute a prize of equal or greater value if the advertised prize becomes unavailable.

10. Taxes

All federal, state, and local taxes on the prize are the sole responsibility of the winner.

By entering, each entrant agrees to these Official Rules and to the decisions of the Sponsor, which are final. The Sponsor reserves the right to disqualify any entrant who tampers with the entry process or acts in violation of these rules. The Sponsor reserves the right to modify, suspend, or cancel the Giveaway if fraud, technical failure, or any other factor beyond its reasonable control impairs its integrity, subject to any applicable law.

12. Limitation of liability

By entering, entrants release and hold harmless the Sponsor and its owners, employees, and agents from any and all liability for any loss, harm, damage, injury, or expense arising from participation in the Giveaway or acceptance or use of the prize, to the maximum extent permitted by law.

13. Publicity

Except where prohibited, acceptance of the prize constitutes permission for the Sponsor to use the winner’s first name and city for promotional purposes without further compensation.

14. Governing law

This Giveaway and these Official Rules are governed by the laws of the State of North Carolina, without regard to conflict-of-law principles. Any disputes will be resolved in the state or federal courts located in Mecklenburg County, North Carolina.

15. Winner list

To request the name of the winner, ask in person at Tabbris after the drawing date, or email hello@friendsandfoils.com.
