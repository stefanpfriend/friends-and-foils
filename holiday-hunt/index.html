01 / THE FIND

Look for a Santa hat.

Find a Pikachu, Bulbasaur, Charmander or Squirtle keychain at an approved host spot. You keep your keychain.

02 / THE SCAN

Check your little find.

Scan its QR code to identify the design and update your browser’s four-design checklist. Scanning is optional.

03 / THE COUNTER

Four finds. One spin.

Bring all four designs for a prize-wheel spin at the shop. One spin per household; rules and prize details apply.

No keychain? You can still join in. Kids 12 and under get one free entry for visiting with a parent or guardian. A free alternate entry is available to eligible participants at the counter. A purchase never improves your chances.

A few good things to know

Before you head out.

Does a scan enter me in the drawing?

No. A scan identifies a tag and updates an optional checklist on your browser. Bring the physical keychain to the Friends & Foils counter, where staff check its unique code and record your entry. Each physical code counts once.

Do we have to find all four?

No. One verified keychain earns a drawing entry. A full set of four different designs earns one prize-wheel spin per household. Your browser checklist helps you remember designs; staff verify the actual keychains.

Can kids take part?

Yes, with a parent or guardian. Children 12 and under get one free entry simply by visiting the shop with their adult. The adult handles entry and prize claims. We do not ask children for contact information on this page.

Do I have to be at the drawing to win?

No. Eligible entries must be logged by Dec 9 at 9:30 PM ET, but the grand-prize winner does not have to be present. Prize pickup is due by Friday, Dec 18 at 5 PM ET. Adults handle minors’ entries and claims.

What can we win?

The grand-prize item and the wheel’s exact prize quantities and values are being finalized. They will be listed in the official rules before the hunt opens. No prizes are awarded by this page.

Do likes, shares or follows earn extra entries?

No. You’re welcome to follow the hunt, but social activity does not count as an entry. No purchase, share, tag or social account is required.
