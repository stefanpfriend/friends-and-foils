NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE DOES NOT INCREASE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. VOID WHERE PROHIBITED.

1. Sponsor

The F&F Holiday Hunt (the "Hunt") is run by Friends & Foils, 1300 South Blvd, Suite D (inside Tabbris), Charlotte, NC 28203 ("Sponsor"). Add legal entity name if F&F operates under an LLC.

2. Eligibility

The Hunt is open to legal residents of North Carolina and the rest of the 50 United States and D.C. Anyone under 18 plays with a parent or legal guardian, and the parent or guardian enters and claims prizes on the minor's behalf. Employees of Sponsor, Tabbris, and participating host businesses who helped hide keychains, and their immediate household members, are not eligible to win.

3. Hunt period

The Hunt starts Friday, November 27, 2026 and ends Wednesday, December 9, 2026 at 9:30 PM Eastern Time. Entries must be logged in person at the shop during shop hours or at the December 9 trade night (7 to 9:30 PM ET) within that window. Sponsor's clock is the official clock.

4. How to enter

  • Keychain entry. Find a Hunt keychain inside a participating host business and bring it to the shop. Sponsor checks the unique code on the keychain and logs one entry. Scanning the QR code alone is not an entry.
  • Free kid entry. Each child 12 and under who visits the shop with a parent or guardian during the Hunt period gets one entry, limit one per child for the whole Hunt.
  • Free alternate entry. Anyone eligible can get one entry without finding a keychain by visiting the shop during shop hours and asking for a free entry, or by mailing a postcard with your name, phone or email, and "F&F Holiday Hunt" to Friends & Foils, 1300 South Blvd, Suite D, Charlotte, NC 28203, postmarked by December 4, 2026 and received by December 9, 2026. Limit one alternate entry per person. Alternate entries have the same chance of winning as any other entry.

5. Limits

  • Each keychain has a unique code and counts for one entry, one time. A keychain already logged does not count again, no matter who brings it in.
  • One prize wheel spin per household for the whole Hunt.
  • Take one keychain per hiding spot. Sponsor may void entries from anyone who removes multiple keychains from a spot, moves keychains to new spots, copies codes, or tries to enter in any way other than these rules allow.
  • Keychains are hidden only inside participating businesses during their open hours. They are never placed on unapproved private property, in public parks, on sidewalks, on the Rail Trail, or on any public infrastructure. Do not search anywhere else, and follow each host business's rules and staff directions.

6. Prizes

  • Grand prize (1): GRAND PRIZE. ARV $225.
  • Prize wheel prizes: single booster packs, booster bundle, Elite Trainer Box from 30th Celebration or Delta Reign, with quantities. ARV per prize $__ to $__.
  • Total ARV of all prizes: $__.
  • Prizes are awarded as listed. No cash substitute and no transfer, except Sponsor may substitute a prize of equal or greater value if a listed prize is unavailable. Winners are responsible for any taxes. Odds of winning the drawing depend on the number of eligible entries received.

7. Drawing

Sponsor will draw the grand prize winner at random from all eligible entries live at the shop on Wednesday, December 9, 2026 at the end of trade night (about 9:30 PM ET). The drawing is recorded and posted on the Friends & Foils Facebook and Instagram. You do not need to be present to win.

8. Winner notification

Sponsor will contact the winner by the phone or email given at entry within 2 days of the drawing. The winner has until Friday, December 18, 2026, 5 PM ET to respond and pick up the prize at the shop with photo ID. If the winner is a minor, the parent or guardian claims the prize. If a winner can't be reached or doesn't claim the prize by the deadline, Sponsor will draw an alternate winner.

9. Publicity

Sponsor may post the winner's first name and last initial. Sponsor will only post a photo of a winner, or any child, with the parent or guardian's permission.

10. Social media

This promotion is in no way sponsored, endorsed, administered by, or associated with Facebook, Instagram, or Meta. You are not required to share, tag friends, or post anything to enter. Likes, follows, and comments on our posts are welcome and do not count as entries.

11. Not affiliated with Pokémon

The F&F Holiday Hunt is run by Friends & Foils, an independent local shop. It is not sponsored, endorsed, or affiliated with The Pokémon Company, Nintendo, Creatures Inc., or GAME FREAK inc. Pokémon and character names are trademarks of their respective owners. The keychains are a free promotional item made by Friends & Foils and are not for sale.

12. Privacy

Sponsor collects your name and a phone number or email only to log entries and contact winners. Sponsor will not sell or share it. If you also opt in to the Friends & Foils email list, you can unsubscribe at any time. Any form for a child 12 and under must be filled in by a parent or guardian, and Sponsor does not collect contact information from children.

13. General

Sponsor may cancel, change, or suspend the Hunt if something outside its control, such as weather, a host business closing, or tampering, affects how it runs, and will post any change on this page. Sponsor's decisions on eligibility and entries are final. By entering, you agree to these rules. Sponsor, Tabbris, and host businesses are not responsible for lost, stolen, or damaged keychains, or for any injury while hunting. This Hunt is governed by North Carolina law.
