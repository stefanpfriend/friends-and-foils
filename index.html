Origin · Why us

Built by a collector, for collectors.

Friends & Foils comes from years deep in the hobby — across every major game, from vintage holos to modern chase cards, grading, and knowing what a deal is actually worth. Now it’s a real shop in South End, Charlotte, inside the Tabbris building at 1300 South Blvd.

No gatekeeping, no hype tax. Just a good counter to buy from, a floor to trade and play on, and cases worth digging through. Come by, or get on the list and grow it with us.
